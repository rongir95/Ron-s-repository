  1. 1
    Make your Fan Card. On Home, tap your avatar (top right) to pick an avatar, a favorite team and a status.
  2. 2
    Open FanZone. Tap Watch now. The match page opens and FanZone opens over it by itself. Close it with X; the FanZone card on the match page opens it again.
  3. 3
    Chat in FanZone. Tap the Message field, type, and press Enter or the send button. Your message appears in the chat with your name and avatar.
  4. 4
    Collapse FanZone. Tap the arrow next to the FanZone title to slide the panel down and see the match page; tap it again to bring it back.
  5. 5
    Vote in a live poll. In FanZone, choose Current poll or Poll V2, then press Start poll. Current poll arrives collapsed: tap it to open, vote for a player, and watch the timer run out to the finished state. Poll V2 slides down with the teams; the arrow collapses it.
  6. 6
    Join the comments. On the match page, tap Comments. View replies, reply, post a comment, and long-press the heart for more reactions.
  7. 7
    Meet other fans. In Comments, tap Futcrunch, Mariana, Sandra45 or MisterFotball12 to open their Fan Card. Tap your own avatar to open yours.
  8. 8
    Manage your comments. Long-press your comment to delete it, or someone else's to report it.
  9. 9
    Lose the connection. Tap the status bar on the match page to turn on No Internet, then try to post.
  10. 10
    Skip to FanZone. In Settings, set Start screen to FanZone to start on the match page with FanZone already open.
