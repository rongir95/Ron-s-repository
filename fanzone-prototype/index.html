Things to try
  1. 1
    Make your Fan Card. On Home, tap your avatar (top right) to pick an avatar, a favorite team and a status.
  2. 2
    Join the comments. Tap Watch now, then Comments. View replies, reply, post a comment, and long-press the heart for more reactions.
  3. 3
    Meet other fans. In Comments, tap Futcrunch, Mariana, Sandra45 or MisterFotball12 to open their Fan Card. Tap your own avatar to open yours.
  4. 4
    Manage your comments. Long-press your comment to delete it, or someone else's to report it.
  5. 5
    Lose the connection. Tap the status bar on the match page to turn on No Internet, then try to post.
  6. 6
    FanZone live chat. Set Start screen to FanZone to open the live chat over the match.
  7. 7
    Vote in a live poll. In FanZone, press Start poll. Pick a team to see the results; the arrow collapses the poll.
